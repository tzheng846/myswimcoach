<objective>
## Goal
Let the coach scope the group comparison to a **part of the swim** — Full swim (default), Stroking
only, Underwater, or a **distance range** — recomputing the metrics over the chosen window entirely
client-side. Fixes the inconsistency that speed spans the whole swim (dive included) while stroke
metrics span only the stroking.

## Purpose
For an A/B experiment the dive is identical in both conditions and dilutes the speed contrast. Letting
the coach compare "stroking only" or "5–20 m" makes the difference they're testing actually visible —
and it's free: the web already has every session's velocity/distance profile + per-cycle data.

## Output
- `web/lib/windowMetrics.js` (pure: window → recomputed REPORT_METRICS values).
- A scope selector on the Groups view feeding those values into the existing headline bars + drill-down.
- `73-04-SUMMARY.md`.
</objective>

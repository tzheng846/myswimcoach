<objective>
## Goal
Ship the per-stroke segmenter choices 59-04 measured: butterfly and breaststroke move to the learned
boundary detector with k=2 pairing; freestyle stays on the wavelet. Add the cycle-regularity check
as a gate, because it is the test that caught a wrong recommendation.

## Purpose
59-04 scored six candidates. Two conclusions survived every check:
- **Butterfly's wavelet is the wrong tool** — F1 0.317, cycle cv 0.218 against a human 0.055, and a
  stroke rate 1.31× the human-derived value.
- **`peakpick` is disqualified despite good F1** — alternation 0.276 vs human 0.056 means its
  "cycles" land on a different phase each time. Its F1 measured boundary placement while the cycles
  it produced were junk.

Only L1 improves butterfly and breaststroke on placement, cycle regularity, AND rate simultaneously.

## Output
`metrics.py` gains a small learned detector with its weights inline — **no new dependency**. The
registry gains two entries. Nothing else in the pipeline changes.
</objective>

      - `p = 1 / (1 + exp(-(X @ _LEARNED_COEF + _LEARNED_INTERCEPT)))`
      - boundaries = `find_peaks(p, height=0.5, distance=0.6 × period × fs)` with the period from

      `_LEARNED_COEF = [0.6976347336, 1.5399329061, 0.2073524188, -0.1574910741, 1.1872824453]`
      `_LEARNED_INTERCEPT = -1.0419426935241078`

    ⚠ **NO sklearn IN PRODUCTION.** Inference is a dot product and a sigmoid. sklearn stays in
    `tools/` for FITTING only. Document that retraining means re-running
    `tools/segmenter_candidates.py` and updating these two constants — they are deliberately a
    constant block, not a loaded artifact, so there is no model file to version, ship or lose.

      `butterfly` and `breaststroke` → `_pair_boundaries(_learned_boundaries, 2)`

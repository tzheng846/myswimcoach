<objective>
## Goal
Make the automatically-computed freestyle stroke rate correct, by fixing the two coupled errors that
currently cancel each other: the swim window (`ip_end` 3.9 s early, `finish` 3.6 s late) and the
cycle definition (every segmenter boundary counted as a cycle).

**Gate:** median auto/human stroke-rate ratio in **0.85–1.15**, AND median |log ratio| strictly
better than today's 0.50, measured on the 12 fully-labeled freestyle sessions.

## Purpose
Every freestyle session in the app and on the web currently displays a stroke rate ~65% too high.
It is the most visibly wrong number the product shows, and 59-01 proved it with ground truth.

Neither half is shippable alone — measured: pairing only lands at 0.761, window only at 2.135, both
at 1.010. That is why this plan bundles two behavior changes, against the phase's usual rule.

## Output
A cyclicity-based swim-window detector and a pairing wrapper, both routed through 59-02's registry;
a dry-run report of what 37 stored sessions would change to; re-baselined pins.
</objective>

  <name>Task 1: Build and score cyclicity-based window detectors in tools/</name>
  <files>tools/window_candidates.py</files>
  <action>
    RESEARCH TASK — nothing here reaches `metrics.py`. Candidates live in `tools/`, per the phase's
    quarantine rule, so a dead end costs nothing.

    Build 2–4 candidate detectors for the swim window, each `(t, vel, fs) -> (ip_end, swim_end)`.
    The reframing to implement (CONTEXT D16): the human marks where CYCLIC STROKING starts and
    stops, not where MOTION starts and stops. Two facts already established that any candidate must
    respect — do not re-derive them, and do not build a candidate that contradicts them:
      - Post-touch drift averages 0.403 m/s, EIGHT times `_BASELINE_THRESH`. An amplitude threshold
        cannot find `finish`. Rhythmicity can: the drift is fast but not periodic.
      - Underwater dolphin kicking IS rhythmic but at the wrong frequency, and in 12/23 sessions
        the first qualifying trough is already the nearest one to the human mark yet still 5–6 s
        early. So `ip_end` is not a trough-selection problem either.

    Suggested starting points (the CWT machinery already exists in metrics.py — reuse
    `_detrend_for_cwt`, `_track_ridge`, the scale grid):
      - ridge POWER envelope crossing a fraction of its own in-swim median, sustained for N cycles
      - ridge FREQUENCY stability (stroke rate is steady mid-swim; drift and kicking are not)
      - envelope of the detrended signal (rhythmic amplitude) rather than raw |vel|
    Per-stroke thresholds are allowed; a single stroke-agnostic rule that works is preferred.

    Score every candidate against the human `stroke_start_s` / `finish_s` using
    `segmenter_eval.score_series` and the same fixture/export path `tools/score_segmenter.py` uses.
    Report per-stroke median and worst |error| for both boundaries, against the current detector's
    baseline (`ip_end` 3.88 s, `finish` 3.55 s).

    Also report, for each candidate, the resulting auto/human stroke-rate ratio WITH pairing applied
    — that is the AC-2 gate, and a candidate that improves boundary error but not the ratio is not
    the winner.
  </action>

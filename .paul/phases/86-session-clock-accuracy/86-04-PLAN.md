<objective>
## Goal
Repair the instrument that voided 86-03's run, using **only data already on disk**, and re-register
the bars for a confirmatory run.

The repair is a change of **detection domain**, split from the **timing domain**:

```
FIND the strikes   → decimated |velocity|, peak-relative threshold   (smoothing kills ring-down)
TIME each strike   → raw |diff(counts)| argmax in a small window     (3.7 ms, no filter in the path)
CHECK the pairing  → encoder interval pattern vs audio interval pattern (clock-offset-free)
```

**This plan does not produce a B1 measurement and must not be read as one.** It spends zero device
time and zero EAS builds.

## Purpose
86-03 collected 8 sessions and 42 taps and had to throw the answer away: **B3 failed at 83.3 %
acceptance against a 90 % bar**, because the encoder tap detector over-triggers (10–28 events for
~5 strikes) and **AC-4's rejection rule is structurally blind to the failure mode that actually
occurred** — it compares audio against frames, two readouts of the *video*, so an **encoder** mispair
leaves both in perfect agreement and is marked ACCEPTED carrying a 180–320 ms residual. 10 of the 35
accepted taps sit more than 50 ms from their own session median.

The detector fires on raw `|diff(counts)|` against a `median + 10·MAD` threshold. A struck wheel
rings, so one physical strike produces a decaying train of crossings, and a 0.5 s refractory does not
collapse it. **The strike is not hard to see — it is hard to see *in that domain*.**

Measured on all 8 void sessions (`scratch/_tap_domain.py`, `scratch/_tap_domain2.py`, read-only,
2026-09-01) — event count as the peak-relative threshold sweeps from 10 % to 60 % of the session's
own maximum:

```
session      10%  15%  20%  25%  30%  35%  40%  50%  60%   audio onsets
VELOCITY  1    3    3    2    2    2    2    2    2    1        5
          4    6    6    5    5    5    5    5    4    2        6
          7    5    5    5    5    5    5    4    4    4        5
RAW JERK  1    9    9    5    5    3    3    2    3    1        5
          4   12   12   11   10   10   10    8    8    3        6
          7   10   10   10    9    9    9    9    5    4        5
```

Velocity holds a **flat plateau on all 8 sessions**; raw jerk never stabilises. That plateau is the
repair. But velocity cannot also carry the *timing*: the velocity peak lags the raw strike by
**+16.3 ms pooled (n = 34, SD 22.6)**, and the per-session means run **−5.0, −2.8, +7.6, +10.5,
+17.5, +25.2, +35.0, +39.4 ms**. Against a 33 ms bar, with a spread that varies session to session
and therefore does not cancel as a constant, timing on velocity would **manufacture exactly the
clock error the test exists to detect**. Hence the split.

The expensive resource in this phase is **device time and an EAS build**, not compute. Repairing the
instrument offline first is the cheap ordering, and the 8 sessions already downloaded are exactly the
corpus needed to do it.

## This is tuning on a spent corpus, and says so

The previous draft of this plan claimed to pre-register its constant before measuring. That claim
would now be **false**, and stating it anyway is the exact failure the phase is built to avoid.

**The sweep has already been run.** The tables above come from read-only probes executed on
2026-09-01, *after* the void run. `TAP_FRAC`, `RAW_REFINE_WINDOW_S` and `PAIR_TOL_S` are therefore
**tuned on this corpus**, in-sample, not pre-registered against it.

What is genuinely pre-registered here is narrower, and still load-bearing:

1. **Each constant must equal what its stated rule produces from Task 1's printed measurement.** The
   rules are written below, before Task 2 enters a number into the file. If a rule produces something
   other than the value anticipated here, **the rule wins and the anticipated value is wrong**.
2. **Every constant is frozen before 86-05's data exists.** That is the pre-registration that
   matters, because 86-05 is the measurement.
3. **This corpus is hereby spent.** It can develop the instrument or it can measure the clock, and it
   has been used for the first. No B1 comes from it, in this plan or any later one.

### The three derivation rules

**`TAP_FRAC`** — peak-relative detection threshold, as a fraction of the session's maximum
`|velocity|`. For each session take the **longest run of consecutive grid values over which the event
count is constant**; that is the session's plateau. `TAP_FRAC :=` the grid value lying inside the
plateau of the **most** sessions; break ties toward the **smaller** value, since a lower threshold
cannot miss a strike a higher one found. *(Anticipated: **0.20**, inside all 8 plateaus, tied with
0.25/0.30/0.35 and identical to them in event count on every session.)*

**`RAW_REFINE_WINDOW_S`** — half-width of the raw-domain search around each velocity candidate.
`:= ceil(4 * max|velocity-to-raw offset| / 0.05) * 0.05`, then **assert** it is below half the
smallest observed inter-strike gap. The 4× covers the offset's session-to-session drift; the
assertion keeps the window from reaching the neighbouring strike. *(Anticipated: max offset 55.7 ms →
**0.25 s**.)*

**`PAIR_TOL_S`** — tolerance on the encoder-vs-audio interval-pattern check.
`:= ceil(2 * max|interval delta| / 0.01) * 0.01`. *(Anticipated: max delta 21.7 ms → **0.05 s**,
derived from only the 3 sessions where the two counts agree, n = 12 intervals — a thin basis, and
Task 3 reports it as such.)*

**If a rule's assertion fails, no constant is set and the failure is this plan's result.** Do not
widen a rule, drop a session, or pick a round number.

<measurement_honesty>
## What may and may not be claimed from the void data

**B3 recomputed on these 8 sessions is a legitimate instrument diagnostic. B1 recomputed on them is
not a measurement.** The distinction is not a formality:

- The repaired detector is derived **from this corpus**, so every number computed on it afterwards is
  **in-sample**. The recomputed acceptance rate is an **upper bound** on the repaired instrument's
  health, not an estimate of it.
- A post-hoc per-session median over this run already gives **−1.79 ± 9.88 ms**, temptingly close to
  zero. **That number may not be quoted, and neither may any successor of it computed here.**

### Expect the repaired instrument to miss taps on this corpus, and do not treat that as failure

Velocity candidates at the anticipated `TAP_FRAC` total **34 against 42 audio onsets** — a ceiling of
about **81 %**, still under B3's 90 % bar. That is a *predicted* outcome of the repair, written down
before Task 3 runs so it cannot be discovered and then rationalised.

The cause is **not established**. Peak-relative thresholding is relative to the session's *hardest*
strike, so a soft strike can fall under the bar — but "the phone heard something that was not a
strike" and "the smoothing swallowed a real one" fit the same evidence. **Task 1 measures which**, by
reporting the velocity amplitude at each unmatched audio onset as a fraction of the session peak.
A soft-but-real strike sits in the few-percent range; nothing-there sits at the noise floor.

Under-detection is the **safe** failure. A missed strike leaves its audio onset with no encoder tap
within `MATCH_WINDOW_S` (1.4 s, against ~3 s spacing), so it is rejected and counted — visible, not
laundered. That is strictly better than 86-03, where over-triggering produced *confident wrong
answers*.

<objective>
## Goal
Add a per-session **auto-stop** to iOS recording: the device (and, in video mode, the camera) stop
themselves N seconds after START, default **20 s**, editable, with a live countdown on the record
screen.

## Purpose
Unblocks **solo** testing. Today the swimmer is the operator: they start, dive, swim 25 m, and must
swim back to press Stop — appending 20–30 s of dead tail to both the velocity trace and the video.
That tail is annotation work, it biases `v95` (which 57-01 just windowed to fight exactly this), and
it makes the tripod + video test scheduled for 2026-08-06 impractical to run alone.

A second, non-obvious benefit: **it repairs the video sync's weakest premise.** The end-anchor origin
(`deviceDuration − videoDuration`, `VideoOverlayScreen.js:53`) assumes camera and device stop
together. Today a failed `writeCmd('STOP')` is caught non-fatal while the device keeps recording,
silently inflating `deviceDuration` and therefore the auto-posted origin. Firing both stops off one
timer is exactly the guarantee the anchor needs.

## Output
One new pref module and edits to two screens in the **`swimnetics-mobile` repo** (separate repo,
user-owned git). No backend, no web, no firmware.
</objective>


## AC-2: Auto-stop fires in video mode, stopping both camera and device
```gherkin
Given auto-stop is enabled at 20 s
When the coach starts a Record-with-Video session and does not touch the phone
Then the camera stops and STOP is written on the same timer firing

```

## AC-3: A manual stop cancels the auto-stop, and vice versa
```gherkin
Given a recording is running with auto-stop armed
When the coach taps Stop before the timer elapses
Then the auto-stop timer is cleared and the stop flow runs exactly once
And no second stop is attempted when the original deadline passes
```

## AC-4: The deadline is visible before it arrives
```gherkin
Given a recording is running with auto-stop armed at 20 s
When 12 s have elapsed
Then the record screen shows the remaining time (8 s)
So a too-short setting is discoverable during the swim, not after it
```

## AC-5: The setting persists and is editable
```gherkin
Given the coach sets auto-stop to 25 s on the recording-config screen
When they force-quit and relaunch the app
Then the field still reads 25 s
And a first-ever launch (no stored value) reads 20 s
```

## AC-6: Auto-stop can be turned off
```gherkin
Given the coach disables auto-stop
When they record
Then no timer is armed and recording continues until a manual stop, exactly as it behaves today
```

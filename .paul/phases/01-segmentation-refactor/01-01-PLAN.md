<objective>
## Goal
Refactor metrics.py to cleanly separate segmentation from metric calculation, use trough-only detection, and auto-trim baseline and post-swim regions. Remove seg_method plumbing from app.py.

## Purpose
The current compute_session_metrics monolith runs segmentation on the full trace, causing false stroke detections in the pre-swim baseline and post-swim wind-down. Unused segmentation methods (peak-anchored, template) and FFT frequency estimation add dead weight. A clean separation makes the code easier to reason about and the segmentation correct by default.

## Output
- metrics.py: 4 functions deleted, detect_phases extended, compute_session_metrics simplified
- app.py: seg_method references and Segmentation settings expander removed
</objective>

<objective>
## Goal
Make the iOS report card tell the truth and show the shape of a swim, not six averages of it.

Three things, in one EAS build:
1. **Correct the time base.** The stored-session path hardcodes 100 Hz; the real rate is ~89.5 Hz
   and lives in `sessions.sample_rate_hz`. Four on-screen consumers are ~11.7% wrong today. (D1)
2. **Replace the Advanced scalar grid with four per-cycle line charts** — DPS, Coast, cycle
   duration and arm peak velocity — each captioned with the session mean or CV it summarizes.
   (D2, D8)
3. **Retire the Data Quality card**, keeping only a magnet-dropout strip above 5%, and stop the
   `cv_isi > 0.80` gate from blanking the whole Efficiency section. (D3, D9, D10)

## Purpose
PROJECT.md lists "Per-cycle charts in iOS app" under Nice to Have, marked *"✓ shipped on web portal
instead"*. That substitution is the gap this plan closes: the coach's poolside device is the phone,
and the phone shows strictly less than the laptop.

D1 is the load-bearing half. Phase 52 established `sessions.sample_rate_hz` as the authoritative
per-session rate and fixed every web consumer; the mobile report card was never touched and still
renders a 47.1 s swim as 42.2 s, with Time-to-Distance understated by the same factor. It is the
same silent-plausible-corruption shape as Phases 51/52/57/59 — a wrong number that looks right.

## Output
- `ReportCardScreen.js` — real sample rate throughout; Advanced section rebuilt on per-cycle charts;
  Data Quality card gone; dropout strip; warning banner instead of blackout.
- `RecordScreen.js` — the same Data Quality removal, dropout strip and banner treatment, so the
  just-recorded view and the saved-session view agree.
- `CycleCharts.js` — NEW. Four small SVG line charts.
- `DataQualityCard.js` — deleted.
- `CLAUDE.md` — the "Sample rate" section corrected (it currently names only the CSV export as the
  iOS gap; there are four consumers).
</objective>

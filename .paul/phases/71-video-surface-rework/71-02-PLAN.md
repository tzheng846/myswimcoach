<objective>
## Goal
Make the **annotate page** the single video hub: it reads the unified `GET /videos` list (so a
web-uploaded external finally appears there), shows **all** of a session's cameras at once, aligns
each **manually** ("point to the same moment" — no push-off, no dive detection), and lets one
**active** camera drive the mark-placing keys. Then **delete** the standalone Videos page and the
report-card "Manage / align" link.

## Purpose
UAT on 71-01 surfaced two things: (1) the annotate page still shows "No video attached" for an
external — it reads only the legacy `video_path`; (2) the coach distrusts the auto push-off/dive
detection that drives "Sync to push-off" and wants manual alignment, with everything in one place.
CONTEXT D10–D13. This finishes the phase: fixes the annotate-side of the store split and replaces
the distrusted auto-align with a coach-controlled one.

## Output
- Annotate page renders a grid of camera tiles from `GET /videos` (phone + externals), one active,
  with per-camera manual two-point align, ±nudge, label, delete, and an "Add camera" tile.
- `VideoPane` + `CameraTile` with **"Sync to push-off" removed**; `CameraTile` reworked into the
  annotate tile (two-point align + active-camera marking wiring).
- `/app/sessions/[id]/videos` route deleted; report-card "Manage / align" link removed.
- `.paul/phases/71-video-surface-rework/71-02-SUMMARY.md`.
</objective>

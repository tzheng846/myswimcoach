<objective>
## Goal
Remove the `steady` / `ramp_up` cycle split from the pipeline (D5) and re-anchor the two
`ratings.py` bands that split was holding up (D15), so that session metrics summarize **every**
cycle and the pillars still discriminate afterwards.

## Purpose
The coach's complaint — *"it feels like the numbers are coming out of nowhere, and for the graphs
the numbers don't reflect what's actually shown"* — is literally true: the charts plot all cycles
while every `mean_*`, `cv_*` and `stroke_count` is computed over the steady subset only. D5 makes
one population. D15 stops that from silently killing two of the four pillars.

## Output
- `metrics.py` with no cycle-phase concept; `stroke_count` = total cycles
- `ratings.py` with re-anchored `cv_arm_peak_vel` + `fatigue_index_pct` bands
- NEW `tools/rampup_impact.py` — committed, reproducible before/after measurement
- Re-baselined test suite with every moved assertion documented
- `61-01-SUMMARY.md` recording old → new for every affected metric
</objective>

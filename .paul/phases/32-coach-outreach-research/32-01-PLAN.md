<objective>
## Goal
Produce a coach-outreach research package for **interest-only** cold emails (gauge interest /
ask for feedback — NOT a sales pitch): (1) a written "ideal target club" qualities rubric with
weighted criteria, and (2) a scored shortlist of real, named swim clubs across greater Southern
California, each with the coach/contact and the evidence behind its score.

## Purpose
The business-model assessment names "zero traction" as the #1 risk. Swimnetics is encoder-only,
shared-unit, periodic-testing — it lands best with mid-sized, performance-minded clubs that have a
national/senior group and a tech-curious coach (see [[competitor_landscape]], [[project_strategy]],
[[business_model_assessment]]). This phase turns "email some coaches" into a prioritized, evidence-
backed target list so outreach time goes to the clubs most likely to say "yes, tell me more."

## Output
`marketing/socal-coach-outreach.md` — one document containing the qualities rubric (Part A) and
the scored club shortlist (Part B). No email copy this phase (user decision 2026-06-16); existing
`marketing/sales-pitch-email.md` is the reuse source if/when an email is wanted.
</objective>

## Research anchors (USA Swimming structure for greater SoCal)
- Greater SoCal is covered by two USA Swimming LSCs:
  - **Southern California Swimming (SCS)** — LA, Orange County, Ventura, Inland Empire.
  - **San Diego–Imperial Swimming (SI)** — San Diego + Imperial counties.
- LSC club directories, club websites, and "training groups" pages are primary sources for
  team size, a National/Senior/elite group, and coach contact. SwimCloud / USA Swimming Times
  reflect competitive pedigree (Sectionals / Futures / Junior Nationals / Olympic Trials cuts).
</context>

## AC-1: Qualities rubric is explicit and weighted
```gherkin

When the document's Part A is read
```

## AC-2: Shortlist is real, scored, and evidence-backed
```gherkin
Given greater SoCal is in scope (LA, OC, SD/Imperial, Inland Empire, weighted evenly)
When the document's Part B is read
Then it contains at least 12 real, named clubs spread across those sub-regions, each with: club
  name, city + LSC (SCS or SI), best-available head/senior-coach name, a contact path (team
  contact/coaching-staff URL or email if public), a per-quality assessment against the Part A
  rubric, a total score/tier (e.g. A/B/C), and a one-line "why reach out" note
```

## AC-3: Honesty about gaps
```gherkin
Given some club facts (exact roster size, coach email, tech usage) are not publicly verifiable
When a data point cannot be confirmed from a source
Then it is marked "unverified" / "estimate" rather than asserted, and the document ends with a
  short "how to verify before sending" checklist (e.g. confirm coach name + email on the team site)
```

    will apply consistently. Keep it tight — a rubric a person can actually apply, not a treatise.

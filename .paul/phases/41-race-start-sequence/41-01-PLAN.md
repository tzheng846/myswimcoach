<objective>
## Goal
Add an optional "race start" cue to the iOS recording flow (`swimnetics-mobile`): a giant
on-screen **3 → 2 → 1** countdown, then a spoken **"take your marks"**, then a **randomized
2–3 second hold**, then a **loud blare** — and recording (BLE `START`, and the camera on the
video path) begins *exactly on the blare*. A persisted toggle on the recording-config screen
controls it; default **ON**. Works over both plain recording and Record-with-Video.

## Purpose
Lets coaches drill race starts with an authentic, un-anticipatable meet cadence while the
encoder captures the swim from the gun. The random hold means swimmers can't time the start.
Captured data begins at the blare, so the buffered session is the race effort, not the setup.

## Output
- New persisted setting + UI toggle on `RecordingConfigScreen` (default on, remembered).
- A reusable start-sequence hook + full-screen overlay component.
- `RecordScreen` start paths gated behind the sequence when enabled.
- `expo-audio` dependency + two bundled audio assets (user-supplied, see checkpoint).
</objective>

    - Exposes `{ phase, run, cancel }` where `phase` ∈
      `null | 'count3' | 'count2' | 'count1' | 'marks' | 'hold' | 'blare'`.
    - `run()` returns a Promise that:
      1. phase 'count3' → wait 1 s → 'count2' → 1 s → 'count1' → 1 s.
      2. phase 'marks': seek-to-0 + play the voice clip; show until it finishes (await
         `didJustFinish` via a playbackStatus listener, with a ~2.5 s safety timeout).
      3. phase 'hold': wait a random `2000 + Math.random()*1000` ms.
      4. phase 'blare': play the horn clip; resolve the Promise immediately at play start
         (recording must begin on the blare, not after it finishes), then clear phase to null
         shortly after.
    - `cancel()`: aborts any pending timers/awaits (track with a ref/AbortController-style flag),
      stops both players, sets phase to null. A canceled `run()` rejects (or resolves with a

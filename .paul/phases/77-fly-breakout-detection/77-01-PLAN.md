<objective>
## Goal
Ship an **arm-cycle-appearance breakout detector** for **butterfly** that places `stroke_start_s` /
`ip_end` at the true Underwater→Swim transition. Gate it per-stroke and behind a confidence check that
**refuses** (falls back to today's `detect_swim_window` `ip_end`) when the ~1 Hz arm structure never
cleanly turns on — so it never ships a confident wrong answer. **Freestyle, backstroke, and
breaststroke are untouched and byte-identical.**

## Purpose
The breakout is the single biggest limiter on the race-phase model (it blanks the underwater metrics
and gates the kick window 75-03 depends on). 76-01 fixed the flutter strokes with a kick-band-
**disappearance** detector; **butterfly cannot reuse it** and is measured WORSE there (4.46 s vs the
2.43 s incumbent) — because *butterfly is a dolphin kick with arms bolted on*: its surface stroke IS
two dolphin kicks per arm cycle (~2 Hz, same band, `Pk_uw/sf ≈ 1`), so the kick band never switches
off. Fly is an **appearance** problem (a ~1 Hz arm pull is ADDED on top of the persistent ~2 Hz kick),
not a disappearance one → a different detector. Butterfly is therefore still on the incumbent
`detect_swim_window` `ip_end` (median |err| **2.67 s** — the bar to beat).

## Validation already done (scratch probes, live DB, 16 annotated fly `stroke_start_s` marks)
The signal is a spectral **reorganization** at breakout — the single underwater ~1.2 Hz kick line splits
into an arm cycle (~0.9 Hz) + two kick beats (~2 Hz) — confirmed by a per-frequency `P_surface/P_uw`
fingerprint (the ratio cancels the CWT 1/f bias; freestyle is the positive control, its kick bands drop
0.19–0.29×):

| band | fly sf/uw | reading |
|---|---|---|
| 0.8–1.1 Hz (arm cycle) | **1.81×** (14/16) | APPEARS |
| 1.1–1.5 Hz (uw kick fundamental) | **0.52×** (5/16) | DROPS |
| 1.5–2.0 Hz (2-beat harmonic) | **2.04×** (15/16) | APPEARS |

Scored against the coach's `stroke_start_s` marks:

| detector | median \|err\| | ≤1 s | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| incumbent (`detect_swim_window`) | 2.67 s | 2/16 | today |
| raw arm-band appearance (0.7–1.3 Hz ON) | weak (only 1.45× uw→sf) | — | **rejected** |
| **`P(0.8–1.1 Hz) / P(1.1–1.5 Hz)` rise-after-low** | **0.35 s** | **12/15** | **WINNER** |

The **ratio** (arm ÷ fundamental) beats raw appearance because it encodes the appearance AND the
disappearance in one scale-invariant number. Robustness (all measured): a 4×4 band-edge grid stays
0.35–0.99 s (**every cell beats the 2.67 s incumbent**); swapping the annotated underwater-start for the
auto `detect_underwater_start` (75-02) gives an **identical 0.35 s** (the production seam holds on fly);
the tail is 2 short-underwater misses (+4.1, +2.0 s) + 1 correct refusal — the same weak-underwater class
the refuse gate handles.

## Output
- `metrics.detect_breakout_fly(...)` — the new arm÷fundamental band-ratio detector + its constants +
  a fly-specific band-power/ratio helper (additive; `_kick_band_power` and every 76 symbol untouched).

  the `P_surface/P_uw` fingerprint + the band-jitter grid, and `--plot` fly traces.
- New tests; suite stays green; **free/back (76-01) and breaststroke byte-identical**.

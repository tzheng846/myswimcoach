## Goal
Mobile QR slate (CONTEXT D7): at plain (encoder-only) record start, the phone generates a
`recording_token`, **displays it as a QR** for an external over-water camera to film, and sends it to
`POST /process` so it lands on the session (70-02). This is the producer half that makes the web
decode (70-03) actually match.

## Purpose
Closes the QR loop end-to-end. Without this, no session ever carries a token and the web pre-fill has
nothing to find (D7 — the reason web-decode-first has no value).

## Output
- `swimnetics-mobile` RecordScreen: token gen (expo-crypto) + QR display (react-native-qrcode-svg) +
  `recording_token` in the /process multipart params.
- Committed in the mobile repo. ⚠ Device-verify rides a paid EAS build (human step).
</objective>

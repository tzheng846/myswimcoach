<objective>
## Goal
Give the coach a **one-tap "Sync to push-off"** on the annotate page's video card: scrub an
external (GoPro/waterproof) clip to the push-off frame, click once, and the velocity trace and the
video lock together. The click sets `video_origin_s = diveSessionTime − videoTime`; the existing
"Save sync" persists it.

## Purpose
Today the web can already attach a clip and nudge it ±0.1 s, but the only automatic origin is the
44-03 **end-anchor** (`sessionDuration − videoDuration`) — correct for the phone (recording and
filming stop together), **wrong for an external camera** that runs on its own clock and never stops
with the encoder. Without a coarse anchor the coach faces dozens of ±0.1 s nudges. Push-off align
replaces that with a single tap, and needs **no computer vision** (CONTEXT D2) — the dive is
auto-detected on the *encoder* (the reliable sensor); the coach only marks the one thing the
encoder can't see, the push-off *frame*.

## Output
- `VideoPane` gains a `pushoffSessionS` prop and a "Sync to push-off" action in the windowed card.
- The annotate page computes `pushoffSessionS` from the dive marker (coach-placed, else auto seed)
  and passes it in.
- No backend, schema, or storage change. Phone end-anchor path untouched.
</objective>

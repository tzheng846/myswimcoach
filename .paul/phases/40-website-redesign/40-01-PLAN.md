<objective>
## Goal
Redesign the Swimnetics marketing site to match the iOS app's look — **Template B
(immersive purple→periwinkle gradient)** — built on shadcn/ui + Vercel design
conventions. This first plan establishes the design foundation (shadcn in plain JS,
the iOS light-purple token set) and rebuilds the **landing core**: Nav, the immersive
gradient Hero with a floating chart card, the restyled SampleChart, and the Footer.
Public pricing is replaced by a **"Request a quote" contact dialog** (name + email +
optional message) that emails leads to tzheng846@gmail.com via a form-to-email service
(Web3Forms). Ends at a human-verify checkpoint so the visual direction is confirmed
before the remaining sections (40-02).

## Purpose
The website currently uses a dark-blue tech theme that no longer matches the iOS app
(Phase 38 light-purple redesign). A coherent brand across the phone and the web is the
credibility signal for the interest-only coach outreach (Phase 32). This plan proves
the new look on the highest-signal surface (the hero) before investing in every section.

## Output
- shadcn/ui wired for this repo (Tailwind v4, JSX — no TypeScript), `components.json`,
  `lib/utils.js`, and base primitives (`button`, `card`, `badge`, `dialog`, `input`,
  `label`, `textarea`).
- A new **light-purple iOS token set** in `globals.css`, additive — the existing dark
  `--color-*` tokens the coach portal depends on are left untouched.
- `lib/site.js` with `WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY` + `CONTACT_EMAIL` — the single config the
  contact form reads. A reusable `ContactDialog` (name + email + optional message) that
  every "Request a quote" CTA opens; on submit it POSTs to Web3Forms, which emails the
  lead to tzheng846@gmail.com.
- Restyled `Nav`, `Hero` (immersive gradient), `SampleChart` (purple), `Footer`, and a
  reordered `app/page.js` landing.
</objective>

<objective>
## Goal
Finish the marketing-site redesign: restyle the remaining sections (Features, HowItWorks)
to the Template-B light-purple theme, replace the public Pricing section with a "Request a
quote" CTA section (reusing the 40-01 ContactDialog), restyle the login page, and retheme
the /faq and /privacy pages to the light theme. After this, every public page matches the
iOS app and pricing is gone sitewide.

## Purpose
40-01 shipped the landing core (Nav, immersive Hero + chart, Footer) but the homepage is
currently just hero + footer, and /faq, /privacy, /login still render in the old dark-blue
theme. This plan makes the whole public surface coherent and removes published prices —
the brand-consistency + "request a quote" goals of Phase 40.

## Output
- Restyled `Features` + `HowItWorks` (light tokens).
- New `RequestQuote` section (gradient CTA card, reuses `ContactDialog`); `Pricing.js` removed.
- `page.js` restored to the full section order.
- `login` page restyled to light-purple (shadcn Input/Button; auth logic untouched).
- `/faq` + `/privacy` rethemed to light (content untouched, EXCEPT the FAQ pricing answer,
  scrubbed to a "request a quote" framing per the pricing-removal directive).
</objective>

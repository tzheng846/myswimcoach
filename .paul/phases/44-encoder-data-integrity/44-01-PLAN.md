<objective>
## Goal
Localize the startup velocity pulse and the firmware↔app sample-count mismatch to a
specific stage of the recording chain (sensor read → RAM buffer → BLE dump → server
processing → physical), using a serial diagnostic that works *while the app records the
same session*. Diagnosis + a usable diagnostic — the targeted pulse fix is a follow-up
plan (44-02) once the stage is known.

## Purpose
Usable encoder data is the core product. Three symptoms remain after the read-path fix:
(1) iOS streaming/received sample count ≠ serial-reported count, (2) the serial-plotter
diagnostic is unusable (it runs only with BLE off and is drowned by debug-log numbers),
and (3) the giant startup pulse persists in the app's velocity output. We cannot pick the
right fix until we know which stage injects the discontinuity.

## Output
- A clean, BLE-coexisting serial trace of the exact recorded RAM buffer.
- Firmware and iOS counts surfaced + compared (mismatch size + direction recorded).
- A written attribution of the pulse to one stage, with evidence → feeds 44-02.
</objective>

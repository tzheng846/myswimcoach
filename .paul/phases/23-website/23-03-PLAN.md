## AC-1: Compare mode
```gherkin
Given a coach with at least two stored sessions
When they pick two sessions (any athletes) on /app/compare
Then both velocity curves render overlaid, aligned at t=0, in two distinguishable theme colors with a legend
And a metric table shows each session's value and the delta, colored by direction of improvement (faster velocity = green, higher CV = red, etc.)
```

## AC-2: Advanced analytics on report card
```gherkin
Given a session report card
When the coach switches the Simple/Advanced toggle to Advanced
Then a per-cycle table appears (cycle #, duration, arm peak vel, trough vel, DPS, impulse, coast fraction)
And per-cycle trend charts (arm peak velocity and DPS across cycles) render
And outlier cycles are visually flagged
And Simple view stays the default and unchanged
```

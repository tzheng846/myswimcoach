<objective>
## Goal
Publish a Privacy Policy page on the marketing site (`web/app/privacy`) that
accurately describes Swimnetics' real data practices — with a children's-data
(COPPA) section reflecting the B2B (club-as-customer) consent model — and link it
from the site footer.

## Purpose
Swimnetics stores performance data and athlete records for minors (age-group club
swimmers). A posted privacy policy is table stakes for any club pilot/procurement
conversation and is the first concrete step toward COPPA/CCPA good-faith
compliance. This addresses the privacy gap surfaced in the 2026-06-13 legal
discussion (see memory `business_model_assessment.md` for adjacent commercial
risks). It is the privacy half of a "legal pages" effort; the companion ToS
(which holds the operative parental-consent clause) is explicitly deferred.

  tokens, `metadata` export).
- Footer link to `/privacy` in `web/components/Footer.js`.

## Established facts (from 2026-06-13 privacy discussion — source of truth for content)
- Operator/controller: Swimnetics. Customer/account holder: the club/coach.
  Athletes are records the coach creates; athletes generally do NOT have logins.
- Jurisdiction emphasis: California (CCPA/CPRA) + federal COPPA (under-13).
- Data actually collected: coach/club account (email, name via Supabase auth);
  athlete records (name, optional age/DOB, `head_waist_m`, stroke type);
  performance metrics (velocity/accel/stroke-rate/cycle data); raw sensor CSVs
  (Supabase Storage `raw-csvs` bucket); device data (ESP32 chip IDs, timestamps).
  Video is NOT currently uploaded/stored server-side (kept local on device) — the
  policy must NOT claim to store video.
- Sub-processors (name them, with policy links): Supabase (DB + storage),
  Railway (API hosting), Stripe (billing — present in backend, not yet client-wired).
- Disclosure mechanism: tokenized parent report pages (`/report/{token}`,
  no parent account, first-name only, no search-indexing).
- Deletion: `DELETE /sessions/{id}` removes the DB row + raw CSV from storage.
- Purpose is narrow: providing the coaching/analytics service + parent reports.
  No selling of personal information; no advertising; no behavioral profiling.

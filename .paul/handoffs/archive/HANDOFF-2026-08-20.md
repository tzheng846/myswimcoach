## What This Phase Does (the one-paragraph version)

Butterfly's Underwater→Swim breakout was placed by the incumbent `detect_swim_window` at **2.67 s**
median error. Phase 76 fixed freestyle/backstroke with a detector that watches the ~2 Hz dolphin-kick
band *switch off* — but that cannot work on fly, because **butterfly is a dolphin kick with arms
bolted on**: its surface stroke IS two dolphin kicks per arm cycle, so the band never switches off,
and the 76 rule measured *worse* than the incumbent there (4.46 s). Fly is an **appearance** problem
instead — a ~1 Hz arm cycle turns on underneath the persistent ~2 Hz kick. Phase 77 ships
`detect_breakout_fly`, which reads the band-power ratio `P(0.8–1.1 Hz) / P(1.1–1.5 Hz)` and marks the
point where it **rises after a sustained low** (the low-run requirement is what skips the push-off
transient).

### Two things measured and REJECTED rather than shipped on faith

- **D5 per-session f0 band refinement:** 2.33 s vs 0.38 s for fixed physical bands. It peak-picks

  a flat 1.0–1.5 plateau.

## Deviations From The Plan (must be reconciled at UNIFY)

1. **Added `_FLY_MIN_CONTRAST`, a gate the plan did not specify.** Writing the refusal tests exposed
   a real weakness: the low/rise thresholds are drawn from the ratio's **own** 20–80 percentile
   range, so a threshold always sits inside the observed spread and a merely *rippling* ratio
   eventually supplies a low run and a "rise". Without this gate the detector could never refuse for
   *lack of signal*, only for running out of window. Value chosen by the measured sweep, not by
   taste.
2. **`_FLY_SCALO_HZ = (0.5, 5.0)`, not the plan's `(0.5, 3.0)`.** The 0.35 s result was measured on
   the 0.5–5.0 grid with 96 scales; changing the geomspace grid changes which frequencies land in
   each band, so the plan's value would not have reproduced the measurement.
3. **Synthetic test tolerance is 1.5 s, not 1.0 s.** The CWT smears a *hard synthetic step* over
   ~1 s at these frequencies, so ~1.1 s of lag is the wavelet, not the detector. The accuracy claim
   that matters is the DB score (0.38 s), not the fixture.
4. **One refusal test asserts with the floor raised, deliberately.** On a synthetic stationary tone
   the contrast lands at 1.35–1.49 — the no-low-run case would pass at the shipped 1.5 floor by only
   0.7%, which would be testing numpy's rounding rather than the detector. Documented in the test.
5. **A real finding worth carrying forward:** the sustained-low-run requirement does **not** by
   itself refuse a stationary stroking trace — the arm cycle's own amplitude modulation satisfies it.
   Only the contrast gate stops that case. The plan's AC-1 assumed the low-run gate would.

## Known Trade-off (deliberate, not a bug)

Session `e50eb628` is a **+4.12 s confident miss** at contrast 1.5; at contrast 2.0 it correctly
refused. Moving from 2.0 → 1.5 gains 3 good detections and re-admits that 1 bad one — net strongly
positive (0.63 → 0.38 s median, 10 → 12 within 1 s). Flagged because it is the one case where the
"refuse rather than guess" convention loses.

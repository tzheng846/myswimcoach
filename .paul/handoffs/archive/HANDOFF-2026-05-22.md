## Critical Context for Phase 6

**Supabase is the right choice** (not Firebase) — data is relational, SQL better for metrics queries.

**Data model already designed** (see STRATEGY.md):
```sql
teams    — id, name, subscription_tier, device_limit, swimmer_limit, coach_limit
devices  — id, serial_number, mac_address, team_id (pre-populated at manufacture)
athletes — id, team_id, name, dob, stroke_type
coaches  — id, team_id, email, role (admin|coach)
sessions — id, athlete_id, device_id, recorded_at, stroke_type, raw_csv_path, processed_csv_path, metrics_json
```

**Device registration flow:** QR on unit encodes serial number → scan in app = claim (not create). `devices` table pre-populated at manufacture.

**No auth yet in the iOS app** — all recordings go to shared Railway backend with no isolation. Phase 6 fixes this.
